🌐 Master Navigation Ecosystem Summary

The Meesho Clone application is structured into 6 autonomous yet interconnected business modules. Users, Resellers, Suppliers, Drivers, and Administrators navigate via targeted journeys, supported by a centralized AppRoutes bridge and a floating Screen Navigator catalog.

💰 Reseller & Fintech 18 Pages
Earnings dashboards, margin config, reseller wallets, instant payout, affiliate partner network, community chat & notifications.
🛒 Customer Shopping 15 Pages
Flutter Luxe home, Category explorer, saree/western listings, rich product details, customer reviews, cart, and payment gateway.
🛵 Logistics & Fleet 9 Pages
Driver mobile console, task assignment, order pickup, live active map route, delivery history, SwiftRoute AI & Meesho Velocity.
🏬 Supplier Hub 9 Pages
Seller web console, inventory & warehouse stock, order dispatching, product upload workflow, category setup & business profile.
🛡️ Admin & Support 6 Pages
Admin master console, catalog approvals, marketing campaign flow, performance analytics, support center & ticket resolution.
🎨 Theme Variations 5 Pages
Digital Curator, Petal Collective, Gilded Pulse, Social Commerce Luxe, and Signal Core theme design ecosystems.
🧭 Global Navigation Systems & Controllers

● FloatingNavigator Modal (App.jsx)

Global overlay accessible from anywhere via window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('open-screen-navigator')). Provides a filtered grid of all 57+ routes categorized by module tabs: All, Reseller & Shop, Shopping, Logistics, Supplier, Admin, Themes.

Global Shortcut & Quick Testing Hub

● NavDrawer (Slide-Over Drawer)

Side menu displaying user profile, active tier status (Gold Tier), and categorized deep-links into: Shop & Catalog, Business & Earnings, Community & Network, Growth & Programs. Includes logout trigger.

Reseller Workspace Context

● AppBottomNav (Persistent Mobile Dock)

Dock bar on mobile/desktop with 6 core touchpoints: Home (/reseller-home), Categories (/explorer), Orders (/delivery-history), Earnings (/earnings-dashboard-1), Profile (/user-dashboard), and Screens (62).

Primary Mobile Experience Nav

● AppRoutes Bridge (handleNav & handleBack)

Normalizes legacy string navigation identifiers (e.g. reseller, cart, inventory, webDashboard, campaignFlow) into canonical React Router paths, allowing seamless component reuse.

🌐 Complete Cross-Module Business Lifecycle (End-to-End Handshake)
1. Supplier (Mod 4): Lists product on /add-product ➔ Verified by Admin on /admin-catalog (Mod 5).
2. Reseller (Mod 1): Discovers product on /reseller-home, sets margin on /share-earn-config, and shares to buyers.
3. Customer (Mod 2): Discovers item on /product, checks /reviews, and completes checkout via /payment.
4. Supplier (Mod 4): Receives order on /supplier-orders, prints label, and packs parcel for courier pickup.
5. Driver (Mod 3): Picks up parcel via /available-tasks, delivers via /active-delivery, confirms OTP delivery.
6. Fintech (Mod 1): Profit margin instantly credited to Reseller /my-wallet ➔ Withdrawn via /withdraw-earnings.
